**Short description (≤ 100 chars)**
Fit the blocks, clear the lines. A relaxing endless block puzzle.

**Description**
Drag blocks onto the 8×8 board and fill full rows or columns to clear them. Clear several lines at once for combos, keep the streak alive for bonus points, and see how long you can keep the board breathing. No timer, no pressure: just one more block.

- Simple drag-and-drop controls, made for phones and mouse
- Combos and streaks reward smart placement
- Your best score is saved on this device
- Stuck? A rescue block can save your run once per game

**How to play**
Drag a piece from the tray onto the board. Complete a row or a column to clear it. The game ends when no piece fits.

**Monetization notes (for the portal form)**
Ads only through the portal SDK: one interstitial between games (after Game Over, when the player taps Play again) and one optional rewarded ad per game (Rescue block). No other ad network, no external requests, no analytics.

**Technical**
HTML5 (Phaser 3, TypeScript). Initial download under 2 MB. No external assets. Works offline after load. Tested on Chrome, Edge, Safari iOS, Chrome Android.
