**Description**
Aim and drop smiling balls into the jar. When two of the same size touch, they merge into a bigger one. Plan your drops, trigger chain merges, and chase the legendary biggest ball before the jar overflows.

- One-finger controls: slide to aim, release to drop
- Eleven sizes to discover, each with its own face and colour
- Chain merges for combo points
- Overflowing? Clear the small balls once per game to survive

**How to play**
Hold and slide to position the ball, release to drop it. Two identical balls merge into the next size. If a ball rests above the red line for two seconds, the game ends.
