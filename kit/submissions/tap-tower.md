**Short description (≤ 100 chars)**
One tap, perfect stack. Time your drops and build the tallest tower.

**Description**
A block slides over your tower. Tap to drop it. Anything hanging over the edge gets sliced off, so every miss makes the next block thinner. Land a perfect drop to keep your width, chain perfects to grow it back and rack up bonus points. How high can you go?

- One-tap controls, instant restarts, 30-second rounds
- Perfect streaks grow your tower back
- Colours shift as you climb
- Fell off? Continue once per game and keep your score

**How to play**
Tap (or press Space) when the sliding block lines up with the tower. Perfect drops within a few pixels snap into place.

**Monetization notes (for the portal form)**
Ads only through the portal SDK: one interstitial between games (after Game Over, on Play again) and one optional rewarded ad per game (Continue). No other ad network, no external requests, no analytics.
